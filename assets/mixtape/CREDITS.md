# Mixtape and title song credits

Full songs by independent bands, released under Creative Commons licences (CC0 / CC-BY / CC-BY-SA) and downloaded from archive.org (url column, which also carries the licence text). Played low on the in-game desk cassette player. Each file was mixed to mono, given a light tape colour (60 Hz high-pass, 9 kHz low-pass, soft saturation) and loudness-matched by `tools/music/build_mixtape.py`; songs over 250 s are cut with a fade. No other changes. Please keep this attribution when redistributing.
