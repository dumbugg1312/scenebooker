# Fonts shipped with Scene Booker

| File | Family (used as) | Designer | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `special-elite.woff2` | Special Elite (`SB Type`) | Astigmatic (Brian J. Bonislawsky) | Apache License 2.0 |
| `anton.woff2` | Anton (`SB Gothic`) | Vernon Adams | SIL Open Font License 1.1 |
| `plex-mono-bold.woff2` | IBM Plex Mono Bold (`SB Mono`) | Mike Abbink, Bold Monday for IBM | SIL Open Font License 1.1 |

All three are free to bundle and redistribute with the game, including in a paid desktop build.
The OFL fonts must not be sold on their own, and their reserved font names stay with the originals
(the game refers to them by its own aliases, `SB Gothic` and `SB Mono`).

- Apache 2.0: https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0
- SIL OFL 1.1: https://openfontlicense.org/open-font-license-official-text/

Special Elite and Anton are the same files diypensacola.com serves; Plex Mono Bold is the upstream release.
